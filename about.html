About Us

Crème & Charm Bakery is a home bakery based in Hanoi, Vietnam. We specialise in handcrafted cakes, pastries and Nigerian treats made with fresh ingredients and lots of love.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday, wedding or simply craving something sweet, we aim to make every order memorable.
